### 1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY

### 2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
Hybrid. The general structure is the canonical Lindblad/GKSL form (most general CPTP semigroup generator) — established, foundational. BUT γ_k(λ) = γ_0(λ/λ_0)² is NOT part of the GKSL theorem — it's a specific phenomenological ansatz / model-derived rate. GKSL does not specify the functional form of rates. The λ² dependence is common (2nd-order perturbation / Fermi golden rule, Caldeira-Leggett) but model-specific.

### 3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
Legitimate. Decoherence is the standard explanation for emergence of classicality. The Lindblad term decays off-diagonal coherences → density matrix becomes diagonal in the pointer basis → indistinguishable from classical statistical ensemble. Genuine quantum↔classical bridge.

### 4. STATUS CALIBRATION
"established" largely correct. (a) GKSL form is rigorously proven, foundational — unequivocally established. (b) Context "explains how superpositions collapse to classical states" is an interpretive overstatement — decoherence gives mixtures, not collapse; does not solve the measurement problem (turns "A and B" into "A or B" but not how nature picks). Reasonable for the equation; context wording should be more precise.

### 5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
Conflation of the general theory with a specific model. Named "Decoherence Master Equation" / presented as canonical, but γ_k(λ)=γ_0(λ/λ_0)² is a specific example, not the general equation. Misleadingly implies all Markovian decoherence follows quadratic coupling dependence. Should rename to reflect the specific model OR present the general form with the rate as a special case.
