### 3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY

The legitimacy of this equation *as a bridge* is the central issue and is subtle.

The equation `ρ_Λ = c²Λ / (8πG)` itself is purely classical; it is an equation of General Relativity and cosmology. It contains no quantum mechanical parameters (specifically, Planck's constant `ħ` is absent). It simply relates one cosmological parameter, `Λ`, to another, `ρ_Λ`.

The "bridge" framing arises entirely from the physical *interpretation* of `ρ_Λ`. The leading hypothesis is that the observed cosmological constant is the manifestation of quantum vacuum energy, i.e., `ρ_Λ = ρ_vac`. This identification is what connects the cosmological measurement to the realm of quantum field theory (QFT).

Therefore, the equation is not a direct formal bridge. It is a classical equation that serves as the target value for quantum calculations of the vacuum energy. The "bridge" is a post-hoc label based on an unproven but deeply important physical hypothesis. The "Known Issues" section of the entry correctly and commendably acknowledges this, stating "The bridge framing... is the speculative element".
