1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
Entropy S has the dimension of energy divided by temperature:
 [S] = [E Θ⁻¹] = M L² T⁻² Θ⁻¹.

Therefore
 dS/dt → M L² T⁻³ Θ⁻¹.

For Onsager‐type systems the entropy-production density is σ = Σᵢ JᵢXᵢ, so each product JᵢXᵢ already carries the same dimension as dS/dt; the multiplier λ must therefore be dimensionless if the sum is taken over the whole system (otherwise λ would absorb any extra factors of volume).

The divergence ∇·v has dimension T⁻¹; to match dS/dt the Lagrange multiplier μ must carry M L² T⁻² Θ⁻¹.

Hence every term inside the brackets has M L² T⁻³ Θ⁻¹, exactly the claimed dimensional signature. The time integral multiplies by T, giving M L² T⁻² Θ⁻¹, but because the variational symbol δ is applied to the integral, the Euler–Lagrange condition is homogeneous and dimensionally consistent. Conclusion: the given signature is correct for the integrand.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
No single, universally accepted "maximum entropy-production principle" exists. The form most often quoted in the literature is the local relation
 σ = Σᵢ JᵢXᵢ  is extremised (usually maximised) subject to constraints,
not the time-integrated functional
 δ∫(dS/dt …)dt = 0.

Dewar (2003, 2005) derives MEPP by maximising path information, obtaining a stationary distribution proportional to e^{ασ}. That derivation does not yield the explicit functional written here, nor does Prigogine's minimum-production theorem, which applies only near equilibrium and gives the opposite variational sign.

Therefore the displayed equation is a phenomenological ansatz, not the canonical form found in the cited papers.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
The "unknown ↔ unknown" label already signals that no concrete regimes are identified. MEPP is usually advertised as linking microscopic stochastic dynamics (statistical mechanics) to macroscopic steady-state behaviour (non-equilibrium thermodynamics). That legitimate bridge is not stated. As written, the bridge description is empty and purely post-hoc.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
(a) The equation itself is not an established result; MEPP remains contested and counter-examples (e.g. Grinstein & Linsker 2007) exist.
(b) The catalog labels the entry "speculative", which is appropriate for both the equation and its interpretation. The calibration is therefore correct even though the supporting text is weak.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
The entry presents an ad-hoc variational functional that is neither derived in, nor consistent with, the main literature it cites; thus it misrepresents what MEPP actually says. Without stating the operative constraints and domain of validity, the principle is unfalsifiable and conflicts with known counter-examples.
