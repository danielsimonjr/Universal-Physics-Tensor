1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY

η_transfer = R₀⁶ / (R₀⁶ + R⁶)
• R₀ and R are lengths: [R₀] = [R] = L
• Numerator: [L]⁶ = L⁶
• Denominator: L⁶ + L⁶  → still L⁶ (same dimension)
• Ratio L⁶ / L⁶ = 1 → dimensionless.

k_FRET = (1/τ_D)(R₀/R)⁶
• τ_D is a fluorescence lifetime: [τ_D] = T
• 1/τ_D → T⁻¹
• (R₀/R)⁶ → (L/L)⁶ = 1 (dimensionless)
• Therefore k_FRET has dimension T⁻¹ (a rate).

The entry lists a single "claimed dimensional signature: [1]" (dimensionless).
That is correct for η_transfer but **incorrect for k_FRET**, whose dimension is T⁻¹.
Hence dimensional consistency is only half–satisfied.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS

Both expressions match the standard Förster-theory forms found in Förster (1948) and Lakowicz (2006):

η = 1 / [1 + (R/R₀)⁶]
k_FRET = (1/τ_D)(R₀/R)⁶

No deviations or missing factors appear (κ², n, J, Q_D are already folded into R₀).
Thus the equations themselves are canonical, not an ad-hoc ansatz.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY

• Quantum side: The derivation of FRET uses second-order perturbative QED between two transition dipoles – definitely quantum.
• Biological side: It is routinely applied to proteins, membranes, chromophore networks, including photosynthetic light-harvesting complexes.

However, in photosynthetic complexes the current debate about "quantum coherence" concerns *electronic* coherence beyond FRET. The quoted equation describes *incoherent* hopping. Therefore calling this a bridge for "Quantum Coherence in Photosynthesis" is a stretch; it bridges quantum electrodynamics with biology, but *not* quantum coherence with biology.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION

(a) Equation: firmly established physics.
(b) Interpretation ("quantum-coherence enhancement of photosynthetic efficiency"): speculative to dubious in light of Cao 2020, Duan 2017, Thyrhaug 2018, which attribute observed oscillations to vibrations, not long-lived electronic coherence.

Thus "speculative" is an appropriate catalog label: established equation + speculative bridge framing.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION

The entry's title and context claim to address "Quantum Coherence in Photosynthesis Efficiency," yet the provided formula is the **incoherent** Förster rate. No term in the equation captures coherence, dephasing, or wave-like transport. The bridge therefore mis-labels the physics it actually implements.
