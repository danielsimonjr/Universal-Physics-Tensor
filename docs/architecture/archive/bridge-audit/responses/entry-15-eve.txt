1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
Take φ ≡ φ_macro(x,t) with some (so far unspecified) physical dimension [φ].

Left-hand side
∂φ/∂t  → [φ] T⁻¹

Right-hand side
a) Deterministic part –Γ δH/δφ
• H = ∫d³x [½(∇φ)² + V(φ)] has dimension energy, [E].
• Functional derivative: for δH = ∫d³x (δH/δφ) δφ, one needs
 (δH/δφ) × [φ] × L³ = [E] ⇒ δH/δφ has dimension [E] L⁻³ [φ]⁻¹.
• With Γ we require Γ (δH/δφ) to match ∂φ/∂t, i.e. Γ must carry
 Γ → L³ [E]⁻¹ T⁻¹ [φ].
Then Γ δH/δφ → ([φ] T⁻¹), identical to the LHS.

b) Noise term ζ(x,t)
Because ζ must have the same dimension as ∂φ/∂t, we again have
 ζ → [φ] T⁻¹.

Noise correlator
⟨ζ(x,t) ζ(x',t')⟩ = 2 Γ k_B T δ(x–x') δ(t–t')
• 2 and k_B T give a factor with dimension [E].
• 2 Γ k_B T → (Γ [E]) → L³ T⁻¹ [φ].
• δ(x–x') δ(t–t') has dimension L⁻³ T⁻¹.
Product: (L³ T⁻¹ [φ]) × (L⁻³ T⁻¹) = [φ] T⁻², exactly the dimension of ζζ.

Therefore every term in the equation carries [φ] T⁻¹, not L² (area).
Claimed dimensional signature “[area]” is incorrect.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
The equation is the textbook Hohenberg-Halperin Model A Langevin dynamics for a non-conserved order parameter, with a Hamiltonian of Landau-Ginzburg form and a Gaussian white noise that satisfies the fluctuation-dissipation theorem. No deviation from the standard form is present. It is, however, a phenomenological ansatz (one postulates that the coarse-grained slow variable obeys this Langevin equation), not a microscopic derivation.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
Model A itself is an effective macroscopic description. It can be obtained from microscopic models using projection-operator or renormalisation-group techniques, but those steps are not part of the entry. As written, the equation only describes the emergent (coarse-grained) level; the microscale appears implicitly through the fitted parameters Γ and the coefficients of V(φ). Hence the “microscale ↔ emergent” bridge is more a labeling choice than a demonstrated connection.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
(a) Physics of the equation: established.
(b) Interpretation as a universal emergence bridge: speculative.
Given that the catalog status refers to the latter, the label “speculative” is defensible even though the underlying equation is standard.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
The dimensional signature is wrong: neither side of the equation has dimension of area; they possess dimension [φ] T⁻¹. This basic mistake calls into question the care with which the entry was transcribed.

6. OVERALL VERDICT
The physics of the equation is sound and canonical, but the stated dimensional signature is plainly incorrect and the bridge interpretation is only weakly justified. These are correctable but non-trivial issues.

OVERALL VERDICT: MINOR-ISSUES
