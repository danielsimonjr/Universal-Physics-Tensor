Standard Lindblad-GKS Markovian master equation. The γ_k(λ)=γ_0(λ/λ_0)² addition is merely a particular rate model; doesn't alter the canonical structure. No algebraic errors.

Lindblad describes loss of phase coherence; suppresses off-diagonal density-matrix elements → predictions indistinguishable from classical probabilities. Quantum↔classical bridge framing defensible — though interpretational, not an additional physical regime.

Status tag acceptable if catalog means "established as the standard decoherence formalism"; wording "collapse to classical states" slightly overreaches.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
