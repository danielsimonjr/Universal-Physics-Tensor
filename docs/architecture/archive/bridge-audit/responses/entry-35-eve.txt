1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
Take the operators Oi to be primary scalar operators of scaling (mass)-dimension Δi in d-dimensional Euclidean space.  In units where ħ = c = 1, a scalar operator has physical dimension

 [Oi] = (mass)^{Δi}.

Therefore the four-point function on the left–hand side is

 ⟨O1O2O3O4⟩  ∼  (mass)^{Δ1+Δ2+Δ3+Δ4}.

Equivalently, in length units: (length)^{-(Δ1+Δ2+Δ3+Δ4)}.

Right–hand side:
• The reduced conformal block gΔ,ℓ(u,v) is a pure function of the dimensionless cross-ratios u and v, hence [g] = 1.
• OPE coefficients C12O , C34O are dimensionless in a unit-normalized CFT.
• The sum is therefore dimensionless.

Thus the RHS carries dimension (mass)^0, while the LHS carries (mass)^{ΣΔi}.  The only way the dimensions can agree is if one factors out the standard kinematical prefactor

 (x12^2)^{-(Δ1+Δ2)/2}(x34^2)^{-(Δ3+Δ4)/2}(x13^2/x24^2)^{…}

which the catalog formula has omitted.  Absent that prefactor, the equation is dimensionally inconsistent with the claimed signature “[1]”.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
Canonical form in the literature (Dolan & Osborn, Rattazzi et al.) is

⟨O1(x1)O2(x2)O3(x3)O4(x4)⟩
 = [x12^{-(Δ1+Δ2)} x34^{-(Δ3+Δ4)}] F(u,v) ,
with the reduced correlator F(u,v) expanded as

 F(u,v) = Σ_{Δ,ℓ}  λ12O λ34O gΔ,ℓ(u,v) .

The entry drops the entire position-dependent prefactor and uses C notation instead of λ, but that is merely relabeling.  Omitting the prefactor, however, is not a harmless shorthand because it changes dimensionality and hides the singularity structure.  Hence the given equation is an incomplete version of the standard result, not strictly the canonical form.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
The catalog says the bridge connects “unknown <-> unknown”.  In practice the conformal-bootstrap decomposition does not bridge two distinct physical regimes; it is an internal consistency condition within a single conformal field theory.  Calling it a “bridge” is simply a catalog convention and does not represent an actual cross-regime relation.  The framing is therefore a stretch.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
a) Equation itself: The conformal-block decomposition (including the missing prefactor) is firmly established, both mathematically and in extensive numerical work—so “established” is appropriate for the equation.
b) Catalog framing: Labelling it a bridge of unknown ↔ unknown is, at best, speculative classification.  So while the physics is established, the catalog’s interpretation is not.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
The formula as written is dimensionally wrong because it omits the necessary position-dependent prefactor.  This is not a cosmetic issue; it misidentifies the object being expanded and invalidates the stated dimensional signature.

6. OVERALL VERDICT
Because of the dimensional inconsistency and the incomplete statement of the canonical equation, the entry contains serious physics deficiencies even though the underlying concept is sound.
