1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
Left–hand side: |cGW – c|/c
• cGW : speed of the GW tensor mode → dimension [L T⁻¹]
• c : speed of light → [L T⁻¹]
• Difference |cGW – c| → [L T⁻¹]
• Division by c (again [L T⁻¹]) cancels the dimensions.
Resulting dimension: [1] (dimensionless).
Right–hand side: 10⁻¹⁵ is a pure number → [1].
Therefore both sides carry the stated signature [1]. The arithmetic is correct.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
The inequality |cGW – c|/c ≤ 10⁻¹⁵ is exactly the standard bound quoted in the GW170817 multi-messenger papers (e.g. Abbott et al. 2017, ApJL 848 L13, eq. (2) in that paper). Literature sometimes quotes 3 × 10⁻¹⁵; adopting 1 × 10⁻¹⁵ is within the quoted 90 % confidence interval and is not a deviation of principle. Hence the formula itself is canonical.

However, the *title* (“MOND – Dark Matter Interpolation Function”) does not match the formula that is actually given. In the MOND / TeVeS literature, the “interpolation function” usually refers to μ(x)=x/√(1+x²) or similar forms that interpolate between Newtonian and deep-MOND regimes; those functions contain the critical acceleration a₀, not the GW-speed ratio. The entry therefore presents a correct inequality but it is *not* the MOND interpolation function it claims to catalog.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
• Gravity side: the equation is a constraint on the propagation speed of the gravitational tensor mode – unquestionably a piece of gravitational physics.
• “Dark-sector” side: TeVeS (and MOND more generally) are put forward as alternatives to cold dark matter; they attempt to *replace* rather than *connect* to the dark sector. The GW-speed bound itself says nothing about dark matter, dark energy, or any hidden sector degrees of freedom. It is merely a test of Lorentz structure in a modified-gravity theory.
Thus using this single inequality as a “gravity ↔ dark-sector bridge” is tenuous. At best it is an *external constraint* on a class of dark-sector-free modified gravities; it does not bridge the two regimes in the sense other UPT entries try to do.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
(a) The inequality itself is rock-solid experimental physics (established).
(b) The *interpretation* that this particular bound constitutes a bridge equation for TeVeS/MOND is speculative.
The catalog’s overall label “speculative” is therefore appropriate, but it should be made explicit that the *equation* is established whereas its *use as a bridge* is speculative.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
The entry’s internal coherence is broken: it is advertised as cataloguing the MOND/TeVeS “interpolation function”, yet the only formula provided is a GW-speed bound that has nothing to do with the interpolation μ(x). Consequently the entry neither documents the intended physical quantity nor actually provides a bridge between gravity and the dark sector. This mis-identification is more serious than a typographical glitch; it confuses two distinct pieces of physics.

6. OVERALL VERDICT
Because the quoted inequality is correct but the entry’s stated purpose (interpolation function) and purported bridge role do not match the equation supplied, the submission has a substantial physics-content mismatch that must be rectified.
