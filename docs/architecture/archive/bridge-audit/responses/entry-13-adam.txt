### 2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
Correct standard trace of the EFE in 4D. Contracting R_μν - ½R g_μν + Λg_μν = (8πG/c⁴)T_μν with g^μν: R - 2R + 4Λ = (8πG/c⁴)T → R = 4Λ - (8πG/c⁴)T. Canonical. Equation established; the Jacobson interpretation is a phenomenological framing, not a formula deviation.

### 3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
Legitimate but subtle. The equation itself has NO explicit information terms (no S, no I). Legitimacy rests entirely on the Jacobson context: δQ = T dS on local Rindler horizons, dS ∝ horizon area (Bekenstein-Hawking), δQ = physical energy-momentum flux. The derivation equates physical energy flow with entropy/information change. Not post-hoc — it's the central thesis of the cited Jacobson 1995 paper.

### 5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
Profound loss of physical content in reducing the EFE to its scalar trace. The full rank-2 EFE = 10 independent equations; the trace is 1. Trace cannot describe vacuum solutions (vacuum trace gives R=0, satisfied by flat Minkowski AND gravitational waves AND Schwarzschild — can't distinguish). Discards shear stress / momentum flow (off-diagonal T_μν). The objection is about physics, not just AST tractability. Authors flagged it, which prevents it being misleading.
