2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
• In Quantum Darwinism the standard qualitative statement is
 I(S : F) ≈ H(S) for fragments F whose size exceeds 1/R_δ,
with redundancy R_δ defined by the fraction of the environment that has acquired (1–δ) of the total I(S : E).  The decay of the *complement* I(S : E\F) is often found to be approximately exponential in fragment size for specific spin-bath or oscillator models, but there is no universally accepted analytic law.
• The catalog’s equation I(S : F_k) = I(S : E) – 𝒪(k^{-α}) with an unspecified exponent α is therefore NOT a canonical formula; it is a phenomenological ansatz.  None of the four references listed writes the relation in that algebraic form.
Conclusion: the equation deviates from the literature and is, at best, an unsubstantiated scaling guess.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
The entry says the equation bridges “unknown ↔ unknown”.  In practice Quantum Darwinism is meant to link the microscopic quantum description (unitary dynamics of S+E) to the emergent classical objectivity of observables.  The present line does not make that link explicit; it merely states a putative scaling for mutual information within a fully quantum framework.  Hence the “bridge” characterization is, at most, aspirational and presently unsubstantiated.
