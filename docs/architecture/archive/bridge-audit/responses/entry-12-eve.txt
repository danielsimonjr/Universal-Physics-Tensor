1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
   Right-hand side
   • ħ has dimension [ M L² T⁻¹ ]
   • ħ² ⇒ [ M² L⁴ T⁻² ]
   • m  ⇒ [ M ]
   • k_B T ⇒ energy ⇒ [ M L² T⁻² ]

   Denominator m k_B T ⇒ [ M ]·[ M L² T⁻² ] = [ M² L² T⁻² ]

   Ratio ħ² / (m k_B T) ⇒ [ M² L⁴ T⁻² ] / [ M² L² T⁻² ] = [ L² ]

   √(2π …) takes the square root: [ L ].
   Left-hand side λ_T is declared a length.
   Result: dimensions match; the equation is dimensionally consistent with the claimed “[length]”.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
   The written form
        λ_T = √(2π ħ² / (m k_B T)) = h / √(2π m k_B T)
   is exactly the definition used in statistical-mechanics texts for the (non-relativistic) thermal de Broglie wavelength of a free particle.  A few authors drop the factor √(2π) by absorbing it into other constants, but the present factorisation is one of the two accepted conventions and is not an error.
   Therefore the equation itself is established, not a phenomenological ansatz.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
   The quantity does mark the boundary between quantum and classical descriptions of a particle gas: when the mean inter-particle spacing exceeds λ_T the gas behaves classically, and when it is comparable to or smaller than λ_T quantum statistics become essential.  In that limited sense it “bridges” the two regimes.
   However the title and commentary invoke the Caldeira-Leggett dephasing length and mesoscopic decoherence, phenomena that depend on system–bath coupling (γ, spectral density, etc.)—none of which appear in λ_T.  Equating λ_T with a decoherence length is therefore a conceptual leap that is not supported by the displayed equation.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
   • Equation itself: established textbook physics (should be tagged “established”).
   • Interpretation as a “Mesoscopic Coherence Length (Caldeira-Leggett)” bridge: speculative at best.
   Consequently the catalog’s overall label “speculative” is defensible only because of the framing, not because of the equation.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
   The entry conflates two unrelated length scales—thermal de Broglie wavelength and the environment-induced decoherence length arising from Caldeira-Leggett friction.  No derivation or parameter (γ, bath cutoff, coupling strength, N-dependence) links the two, so calling λ_T a “Caldeira-Leggett dephasing length” is physically unjustified.
