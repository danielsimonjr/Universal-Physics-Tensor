1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
Definition normally used in the asymptotic–safety literature:
g(k) ≡ Gk k² and λ(k) ≡ Λk / k², where k is the RG momentum scale, Gk the (running) Newton constant, and Λk the (running) cosmological constant.
• G has dimension [G]=L²/M T². Multiplying by k² (dimension L⁻²) makes g(k) dimensionless.
• Λ has dimension L⁻², dividing by k² (also L⁻²) makes λ(k) dimensionless.
The β–functions are defined as
β_g ≡ k ∂g/∂k and β_λ ≡ k ∂λ/∂k.
Because k carries the same length dimension in numerator and denominator, β_g and β_λ are also dimensionless.

Right-hand side: every term is a product of powers of the dimensionless variables g and λ multiplied by dimensionless numerical coefficients A … F.
Therefore RHS has dimension 1.
Left-hand side: β_g, β_λ are dimensionless ⇒ dimension 1.

Conclusion: both sides have the same (dimensionless) physical dimension. The claimed dimensional signature “[1]” is therefore satisfied.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
• Canonical FRG form (Einstein–Hilbert truncation) is
β_g = (2 + η_N) g
β_λ = (η_N – 2) λ + ½ g B₁(λ) / [1 – g B₂(λ)]          (Reuter 1998, eqs. (3.18), (3.19))
where η_N is the anomalous dimension of Newton’s coupling and the B_i depend on the regulator.

• The entry’s equations are a polynomial Taylor expansion of those canonical expressions around g = 0, λ = 0, with η_N itself expanded as η_N = A g + B g² – C g λ + … .

• Such a low-order polynomial is widely used in first-pass studies but it is not unique and the coefficients A…F are scheme-, regulator- and gauge-dependent; they are not universal.

Hence the equations are schematic, not the standard closed FRG form; that deviation is acceptable as an ansatz but must be labeled explicitly as such (and the entry currently does not).

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
The catalog lists the bridge as “unknown ↔ unknown”. In other words it does not claim to connect two identifiable regimes (e.g. quantum ↔ classical); it merely states that the entry is in the catalog. No genuine bridge between established regimes is exhibited, so the “bridge” framing is effectively empty.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
(a) Physics of the β–functions: It is mainstream research that a non-Gaussian fixed point may render gravity asymptotically safe; the existence of that fixed point is supported by many truncation studies but is still unproven and untested experimentally.
(b) Catalog framing: claiming a UV-complete theory of quantum gravity is speculative until the fixed point’s existence and predictivity are established.

Therefore the “speculative” label is appropriate.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
The entry treats the coefficients A, B, C, D, E, F as if they were meaningful numbers independent of truncation, regulator and gauge, whereas in reality they vary across schemes. Without specifying the truncation and regulator, the β-functions are not well-defined and cannot be used for quantitative work or for locating a fixed point.

6. OVERALL VERDICT
SERIOUS-ISSUES — the equations are dimensionally consistent and qualitatively recognizable, but the lack of a specified truncation/regulator, the implication of universality of the coefficients, and the absence of an actual “bridge” make the entry scientifically unreliable in its current form.
