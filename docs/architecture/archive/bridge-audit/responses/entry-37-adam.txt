### 1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY

The dimensional consistency of the formula is correct.

-   **Left-Hand Side (LHS):** The term `Δt` represents a time delay, so its dimension is `[time]`, or `[T]`.

-   **Right-Hand Side (RHS):** The RHS is `(2GM/c³)·ln(R_far/R_near)`. Let's analyze the dimensions of each component in SI base units (M, L, T for mass, length, time):
    -   `G` (Gravitational constant): `[M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]`
    -   `M` (Mass): `[M]`
    -   `c` (Speed of light): `[L T⁻¹]`
    -   `R_far`, `R_near` (Distances): `[L]`

-   **Dimensional Arithmetic:**
    1.  The argument of the natural logarithm, `R_far / R_near`, has dimensions `[L] / [L] = [1]`. It is a dimensionless quantity, as required for the argument of a transcendental function. The logarithm of a dimensionless number is itself dimensionless.
    2.  The prefactor is `GM/c³`. Let's compute its dimensions:
        `[G M / c³] = ([M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²] · [M]) / [L T⁻¹]³`
        `= [L³ T⁻²] / [L³ T⁻³]`
        `= [L³⁻³ T⁻²⁻⁽⁻³⁾]`
        `= [L⁰ T¹] = [T]`

-   **Conclusion:** The dimension of the RHS is `[T] · [1] = [T]`. This matches the LHS dimension `[T]`. The claimed dimensional signature `[time]` is correct.

### 2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS

The formula provided is the correct, standard, leading-order approximation for the Shapiro time delay as derived from General Relativity (GR).

-   The equation `Δt = (2GM/c³)·ln(R_far/R_near)` is a well-established result for the excess round-trip time for a light signal passing near a spherically symmetric mass `M`. It is not a phenomenological ansatz.
-   To be more precise, in the broader context of the Parameterized Post-Newtonian (PPN) framework (referenced via Will 1981/2014), the time delay is proportional to `(1+γ)/2`, where `γ` is the PPN parameter measuring the curvature of spacetime produced by a unit rest mass. In standard General Relativity, `γ = 1`, which yields the prefactor of `(1+1)/2 * (2GM/c³) = 2GM/c³` as written.
-   The cited Bertotti et al. 2003 measurement using the Cassini spacecraft confirmed `γ` to be 1 to within parts in 10⁵, providing extremely strong experimental validation for the GR form of this equation.

### 3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY

The entry fails completely as a "bridge equation".

-   The entry claims to bridge `unknown <-> unknown`. This is, by definition, not a bridge. A bridge equation must connect two distinct, specified physical regimes or theories.
-   The Shapiro delay is a prediction *within* a single theory: General Relativity. It describes an effect of curved spacetime on the propagation of light. It is not a bridge connecting GR to another regime (e.g., quantum mechanics). It represents a correction to the null-geodesics of flat spacetime (Special Relativity/Newtonian), but it is not typically framed as a "bridge equation" in the UPT sense.
-   The "Known Issues" section makes the problem clear: this entry is a relic of a previous, abandoned Varying Speed of Light (VSL) model. The authors replaced the non-viable VSL formula with the Shapiro delay formula but failed to update the entry's core purpose and metadata. The Shapiro delay does not serve the function of the original VSL proposal (e.g., solving cosmological puzzles), so the substitution is not a simple correction but a fundamental change of subject.
