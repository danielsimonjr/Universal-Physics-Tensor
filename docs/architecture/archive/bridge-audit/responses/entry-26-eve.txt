2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
• The factor ν₀ exp[−(2/ħ)∫ dx √{2m(V−E)}] is exactly the textbook WKB expression for a 1-D tunnelling probability per unit time (Landau & Lifshitz QM §50) multiplied by an attempt frequency.
• The entry adds an undifferentiated f(T,pH,EM) to absorb every biochemical correction. That factor is not part of the canonical physics expression; it is a phenomenological ansatz.
• Minor syntax issue: a stray comma appears before dx, and ν₀ is left undefined, but the mathematical content is otherwise standard.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
What is really being bridged is "quantum proton tunnelling in a hydrogen bond" (microscopic quantum regime) → "observed point-mutation rate in DNA replication" (mesoscopic biochemistry). That is a genuine quantum-biology connection; however the catalog leaves both sides as "unknown", so the bridge description is ill-specified. The physics part of the formula only covers the quantum side; the biological side is shunted into the ad-hoc factor f. Hence the bridge, as written, is only partial.

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
(a) The WKB rate formula itself is established physics.
(b) Using it as the dominant predictor of DNA mutation frequency, after sweeping enzyme fidelity and post-replication repair into f, is unverified and controversial. Therefore calling the entry "speculative" is appropriate; "highly-speculative" would also be defensible but "speculative" is not an underestimate.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
With reasonable barrier parameters (m_p, V≈0.4 eV, width≈1 Å) the WKB exponent is ≈28, giving P≈10⁻¹². With a vibrational attempt rate ν₀≈10¹³ s⁻¹ this predicts Γ≈10 s⁻¹ per base pair—nine orders of magnitude larger than the observed 10⁻⁸–10⁻¹⁰ mutations per bp per replication. The entry leaves this discrepancy to an undefined catch-all factor f, so it lacks quantitative predictive power and is not falsifiable in its present form.

6. OVERALL VERDICT
The underlying tunnelling formula is correct, but the bridge framing is inadequately specified and fails by many orders of magnitude without an ad-hoc fudge factor. Significant revision is required.
