1. DIMENSIONAL CONSISTENCY
   Symbols: G [L³ M⁻¹ T⁻²], M [M], b [L], c [L T⁻¹], α (deflection angle) is taken to be dimensionless (the catalog’s “[1]” signature).
   Right-hand side
      4 G M / (b c²) → (L³ M⁻¹ T⁻²)(M) / (L · L² T⁻²)
                       = L³ / T²  ·  1 / (L³ T⁻²)
                       = 1 (dimensionless).
   Hence RHS is dimensionless, matching the LHS (an angle) and the catalog’s “[1]”. Dimensional arithmetic checks out.

2. CANONICAL CORRECTNESS
   The displayed relation α = 4GM/(b c²) is exactly the first-post-Newtonian (weak-field, small-angle) light-deflection formula derived from the Schwarzschild metric and quoted in standard GR texts (e.g. Carroll §8.5). No constants or factors are missing; the dependence on impact parameter b and total mass M is correct. Therefore the entry reproduces the canonical form, not an ad-hoc ansatz.

3. BRIDGE LEGITIMACY
   • Origin side: The derivation starts from GR (null geodesics in Schwarzschild).
   • Target side: The leading potential term uses the Newtonian GM/r potential, and the limit b ≫ r_s corresponds to the Newtonian weak-field regime.
   • Newtonian gravity alone gives only half the stated value (α_N = 2GM/(b c²)); GR supplies the extra factor 2. Thus the equation explicitly shows how a Newtonian quantity (the potential) is amended by relativistic curvature effects. Calling it a bridge between “Newtonian gravity ↔ GR” is reasonable, though the flow is one-way (it demonstrates the insufficiency of Newtonian theory rather than interpolating between two valid regimes).

4. STATUS CALIBRATION
   (a) Equation: Empirically verified to <10⁻⁴ precision by modern VLBI and gravitational-lens observations; solidly established.
   (b) Framing: Labeling it a bridge is interpretive but defensible. Therefore “established” is an appropriate catalog status.

5. STRONGEST OBJECTION
   The entry never defines the symbol b. Without stating that b is the impact parameter (≈ the closest approach distance measured perpendicular to the asymptotic light path) readers may mis-identify it with a radial coordinate or stellar radius. This definition is essential for quantitative use.

6. OVERALL VERDICT: The physics is correct and the status tag is appropriate; only minor clarity issues (symbol definition) are present.
