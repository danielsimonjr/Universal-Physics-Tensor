## 1. Context

UPT v0.3.5 (tag `v0.3.5`, commit `7786027`, 2026-05-14) shipped the **numerical-contraction backend**: a `TensorEngine` interface with `Float64ReferenceEngine` (zero-dep default) and `MathTSEngine` (over `@danielsimonjr/mathts-tensor`), AST→engine lowering, two-way numerical partial derivatives, `InverseMetricInconsistencyWarning`, and BE-37 Shapiro-delay eikonal end-to-end at relative error 7.3×10⁻¹⁰.

**v0.4.0 is the first release where the connection-layer machinery EXISTS TO ENCODE NEW BRIDGES.** The north stars are explicit: UPT *verifies bridge equations*, builds the mathematical framework *with MathTS, tensors, threejs*, toward an *integrated scientific environment* (TensorJS). v0.4.0 adds two canonical Einstein-1915 GR-flavored bridges — **gravitational lensing** (α = 4GM/(bc²), Eddington 1919) and **Mercury perihelion precession** (Δφ = 6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²), Le Verrier 1859 / Einstein 1915) — that the **connection layer enables**. The connection layer itself (Christoffel `Γ^λ_μν` + covariant derivative `∇_μ`, both symbolic AST + numerical) and the Schwarzschild geodesic integrator are the **infrastructure** the new bridges run on, not the headline deliverable.

Two MathTS-side deliverables ride alongside, reinforcing the "mathematical framework with MathTS" pillar: forward-mode AND reverse-mode automatic differentiation ship as **a first-class new MathTS package** `@danielsimonjr/mathts-autograd` (sister to v0.3.5's `mathts-tensor`); UPT's `MathTSEngine` becomes a thin adapter over it. At release time, MathTS becomes the registry default engine.

Per the v0.3.5-refreshed roadmap: v0.4.0 ships the connection layer + two GR bridges + MathTS AD package; v0.5.0+ adds curvature, the Faraday-cascade BREAKING refactor, real MathTS perf optimizations, and threejs visualization bootstraps.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

1. **Two new Einstein-GR bridges in the catalog:**
   - **BE-?? Gravitational Lensing** (Eddington 1919; modern radio interferometry). Symbolic encoding: null-geodesic deflection-angle calculation in Schwarzschild. Closed-form (weak-field): `α = 4GM/(bc²)` where `b` is the impact parameter. Numerical: RK4-integrate the null geodesic, accumulate the angle change. Acceptance: numerical α matches `4GM/(bc²)` to **±10⁻⁹ relative** in the weak-field domain (impact parameter `b ≫ r_Schwarz`).
   - **BE-?? Mercury Perihelion Precession** (Le Verrier 1859, Einstein 1915). Symbolic encoding: per-orbit precession of the perihelion of a bound timelike geodesic in Schwarzschild. Closed-form (weak-field): `Δφ_per_orbit = 6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²)` where `a` is the semi-major axis, `e` the eccentricity. Numerical: RK4-integrate the timelike geodesic for N orbits, extract the secular perihelion drift. Acceptance: numerical drift over N orbits matches `N × 6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²)` to **±10⁻⁹ relative** in the weak-field domain (orbit radius `r ≫ r_Schwarz`).

   Both bridges are canonical Einstein-1915 GR tests; both *are* bridges between Newtonian and GR regimes; both will become permanent entries in the 40-bridge catalog (numbers picked at implementation time from `src/bridges/index.ts`'s next-free range).

2. **Connection-layer machinery (the infrastructure):**
   - **`covariant-derivative` AST node**: a new `ExprNode` kind parallel to v0.3.0's `tensor-partial-derivative`. Shape `{ kind: 'covariant-derivative', of, wrt, wrtIndex, gLower, gInverse }`. Dedicated validator case with internally-consumed metric indices (§7).
   - **`christoffel()` helper**: builder function `christoffel(gLower, gInverse, λ, μ, ν, xCoord): ExprNode` returning a composite tree `(1/2)·g^λρ·(∂_μ g_ρν + ∂_ν g_ρμ − ∂_ρ g_μν)` built from existing v0.3.0 nodes. Parallels v0.3.0 `raise()` / `lower()`.
   - **`derivativeStrategy` field on `metric-tensor`**: optional `'computed' | 'zero' | 'supplied'`, defaults `'computed'`. Closes the v0.3.0-deferred `varying` question.
   - **Schwarzschild geodesic integrator**: extension of v0.3.5's `null-ray-integrator` to the 8-dim geodesic state vector `[x^μ, dx^μ/dτ]`. Used by BOTH bridges. Integration domain restricted to **r ≥ 3·r_Schwarz** (Adam + Eve H2).

3. **MathTS AD as a first-class new package: `@danielsimonjr/mathts-autograd`.** A new MathTS workspace package depending on `@danielsimonjr/mathts-tensor` (v0.1.0+). Ships forward-mode AD (dual numbers — `DualTensor` wrapper) and reverse-mode AD (tape + backward — `Tape` class). Exposes `forwardGrad(fn, x): { value, jacobian }` and `reverseGrad(fn, x, cotangent?): { value, gradient }` as public functions. Version 0.1.0 at release.

4. **AD on `TensorEngine` interface**: two optional methods, `forwardGrad?` and `reverseGrad?` (shapes pinned in §6). **`Float64ReferenceEngine`** implements them in-house (zero-dep dual-number / tape implementation). **`MathTSEngine`** adapts via the new `mathts-autograd` package. Both engines pass an identical AD-conformance test layer.

5. **`InverseMetricInconsistencyWarning` extension**: a new sub-rule fires when the same metric name appears in one AST with **conflicting `derivativeStrategy` values** (Eve M2).

6. **MathTSEngine becomes the registry default** at release time. Prerequisite: the owner manually publishes BOTH `@danielsimonjr/mathts-tensor` (still v0.1.0 — deferred from v0.3.5) AND `@danielsimonjr/mathts-autograd` (v0.1.0 — new in v0.4.0) to npm. UPT then promotes the dep from `optionalDependencies` to `dependencies` and flips the registry default. Honest framing: this is a code-path-signal + dependency-shape change; v0.4.0 makes the *bridge of perf substrate* available — real perf optimizations remain v0.5.0+ work.

7. **`Part-IX-Connection-Layer.md`** spec section following the Part-VIII pattern (TENSOR-RULE markers, spec↔impl drift-guard test).

- **Friedmann-flavored cosmology bridges**: v0.5.0+ (curvature-adjacent — needs Einstein field equations).

## 3. Approach

Three pillars, each grounded in an existing v0.3.0 or v0.3.5 precedent:

1. **Bridges drive the work.** v0.3.0/v0.3.5 used BE-37 as their worked-example bridge — the metric layer and the numerical backend earned their existence by *encoding a real bridge*. v0.4.0 does the same with **two** bridges (lensing + perihelion), both leveraging the new connection-layer machinery. The Schwarzschild geodesic integrator and the new AST/AD machinery are *infrastructure*, not the headline.

2. **`∇_μ` as a node, `Γ` as a helper.** Mirrors v0.3.0's "fundamental operators get dedicated AST kinds; composite formulas are helpers." Part-VIII §VIII.8 hints at this exactly: *"v0.4.0 will swap `pderiv` for the covariant derivative `∇_μ`… only the operator changes."* Christoffel `Γ` is a composite formula (already provable composable from v0.3.0 nodes per §VIII.9 + the preview test); helper.

3. **AD lives in MathTS.** "Build the mathematical framework with MathTS, Tensors, and threejs" is the north star; AD is mathematical framework, so it goes in MathTS. The new `@danielsimonjr/mathts-autograd` package is the first-class home. UPT's `MathTSEngine` adapts it cleanly; `Float64ReferenceEngine` keeps a parallel in-house implementation only because of UPT's zero-dep-default posture. The AD interface on `TensorEngine` is the shared contract.

## 4. AST extension

### Existing node types (v0.3.5, unchanged shapes except one additive optional field)

```typescript
// src/dimensional/metric-validators.ts — MetricTensorNode gains one optional field
export interface MetricTensorNode {
  readonly kind: 'metric-tensor';
  readonly name: string;
  readonly indices: ReadonlyArray<TensorIndex>;
  readonly signature: string;
  readonly dim: Dimension;
  readonly derivativeStrategy?: 'computed' | 'zero' | 'supplied';  // NEW in v0.4.0; defaults 'computed'
}
```

`derivativeStrategy` defaults to `'computed'` — every v0.3.x AST is a valid v0.4.0 AST. Numerical lowering branches on this field:
- `'computed'`: lowering uses `engine.forwardGrad?` on the metric function (the default).
- `'zero'`: constant metric — `∂g = 0`, `Γ = 0`, `∇_μ = ∂_μ`.
- `'supplied'`: lowering looks up `inputs.metricDerivatives.get(`${gName}/${coordLabel}`)` (parallel to v0.3.5's `inputs.derivatives` for `'symbolic'` tensor-symbols).

### The new `covariant-derivative` node

```typescript
// src/dimensional/connection-validators.ts (new module — parallels metric-validators.ts)
export interface CovariantDerivativeNode {
  readonly kind: 'covariant-derivative';
  readonly of: unknown;           // ExprNode — typed unknown to break the module cycle
  readonly wrt: unknown;          // ExprNode — used for its dim only (same as pderiv)
  readonly wrtIndex: CovariantIndex;
  readonly gLower: MetricTensorNode;
  readonly gInverse: MetricTensorNode;
}
```

The `gLower`/`gInverse` slots are full child nodes — their free indices are consumed internally by the Christoffel formula's contractions and never propagated upward (§7 H1 fix).

### `NumericalInputs` extension

```typescript
export interface NumericalInputs {
  // ... existing v0.3.5 fields ...
  readonly metricDerivatives?: ReadonlyMap<string, NestedArray>;  // keyed `${metricName}/${coordLabel}`
}
```

## 5. The `christoffel()` helper

```typescript
// src/dimensional/connection.ts (new module — parallels metric.ts)
export function christoffel(
  gLower: MetricTensorNode,
  gInverse: MetricTensorNode,
  upper: string,         // λ
  lowerA: string,         // μ
  lowerB: string,         // ν
  xCoord: TensorSymbolNode,  // coordinate field, role='coordinate'
): ExprNode;

export function isChristoffelSymmetric(node: ExprNode): boolean;
```

The fresh contracted label `ρ` (between `g^λρ` and the `∂g` terms) is generated deterministically using the v0.3.0 `freshLabel(<base>, <taken>)` scheme. Symmetry of Γ in `μν` is asserted by the explicit `isChristoffelSymmetric` checker rather than baked into the validator (callers run it when they want).

## 6. AD interface on `TensorEngine`

Two **optional** methods added:

```typescript
export interface TensorEngine {
  // ... existing v0.3.5 methods ...

  /**
   * Forward-mode AD via dual numbers. Returns value + full Jacobian.
   * jacobian.shape = [...value.shape, ...x.shape] (row-major flatten);
   * jacobian[i_y..., i_x...] = ∂y[i_y...] / ∂x[i_x...].
   */
  forwardGrad?(
    fn: (x: EngineTensor) => EngineTensor,
    x: EngineTensor,
  ): { value: EngineTensor; jacobian: EngineTensor };

  /**
   * Reverse-mode AD via tape + backward. Returns value + vector-Jacobian product.
   * gradient.shape === x.shape;
   * gradient[i_x...] = Σ cotangent[y...] · ∂value[y...] / ∂x[i_x...].
   * cotangent defaults to ones-like(value) when value is scalar; required otherwise.
   */
  reverseGrad?(
    fn: (x: EngineTensor) => EngineTensor,
    x: EngineTensor,
    cotangent?: EngineTensor,
  ): { value: EngineTensor; gradient: EngineTensor };
}
```

**Implementations:**

- **`Float64ReferenceEngine.forwardGrad`/`reverseGrad`**: in-house, zero-dep. Dual numbers extend every existing engine op to operate on `{ primal, tangent }` pairs; tape records ops + backward closures, replays in reverse. Standard textbook AD over the existing `Float64Tensor` types.
- **`MathTSEngine.forwardGrad`/`reverseGrad`**: thin adapter — delegates to `@danielsimonjr/mathts-autograd`'s `forwardGrad`/`reverseGrad` public functions. MathTS owns the implementation; UPT's adapter wraps the engine tensors → mathts-tensor `Tensor`s, calls the mathts-autograd function, wraps the result back.

**Convention (Adam L2):** the `christoffel()` lowering pass requests the **full** `∂g` Jacobian in ONE `forwardGrad?` call (using the metric function `x → g(x)` as `fn`). Component-by-component is forbidden by the design. The conformance suite tests that a known-derivative function `fn(x) = x · x` returns the correct full Jacobian in one call.

**Closure convention for non-coordinate parameters:** the lowering pass constructs `fn` from the `metric-tensor` AST + the supplied `NumericalInputs`. Non-coordinate parameters that the metric depends on (e.g., `M_kg` for Schwarzschild) are **captured in `fn`'s closure** by the lowering pass — they do not appear as additional arguments to `forwardGrad?`. The only variable seen by the AD engine is the coordinate tensor `x`.

## 7. Validator changes

### Dedicated `covariant-derivative` case (Eve H1 fix)

The validator's `infer()` recursion gains a `case 'covariant-derivative'`. It validates:
- `wrtIndex.variance === 'lower'` (mirrors pderiv).
- `gLower.indices` both `'lower'`; `gInverse.indices` both `'upper'`; matching `signature`.
- `node.of` validated via `inferArgLocal()` (free indices merged into the result).
- `gLower` and `gInverse` validated **without propagating their free indices upward** — consumed internally by Christoffel contractions. (Mirrors `tensor-partial-derivative`'s `pderiv-ignores-wrt-own-indices` rule.)

Output:
- `dim = divide(of.dim, wrt.dim)` (same as pderiv).
- `freeIndices = of.freeIndices ∪ { wrtIndex.label: lower }` (matches `∂_μ V^ν` and `Γ^ν_μλ V^λ` by construction — Adam verified).

### Duplicate-`derivativeStrategy` warning (Eve M2 fix)

A post-walk pass detects same-name metric-tensor nodes with conflicting `derivativeStrategy`. Emits a `severity: 'warning'` Violation: `DuplicateDerivativeStrategyWarning: metric '<name>' is tagged '<a>' at one site and '<b>' at another`. Deduplicated by `(name, strategyA, strategyB)`. Same pattern as v0.3.5's `InverseMetricInconsistencyWarning`.

## 8. Worked examples — the two new bridges

The Schwarzschild geodesic integrator and connection-layer machinery are SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE. Both bridges sit on top of it.

### BE-?? Gravitational Lensing (primary)

**Bridge identity.** Newtonian-point-mass-gravity ↔ GR weak-field. Eddington (1919) measured the deflection of starlight by the Sun during the solar eclipse — α ≈ 1.75 arcsec per solar radius for grazing rays — confirming Einstein's 1915 prediction `α = 4GM/(bc²)` (twice the Newtonian-corpuscular prediction `2GM/(bc²)`). Modern measurements via radio interferometry confirm to ~10⁻⁴ precision.

**Symbolic encoding.** `src/bridges/equations/be-XX-gravitational-lensing.ts`:
- Schwarzschild inverse metric `g^μν` (already shipped via the BE-37 reuse — same `g_inverse_eikonal` shape).
- The deflection angle is an integral along the null geodesic: `α = 2·∫_b^∞ dφ/dr · dr − π` (one of several equivalent forms). In UPT AST terms: the integrand involves `g^μν` and a `tensor-symbol` for the conserved angular momentum `L` along the geodesic.
- Closed-form scalar: `α = 4·G·M/(b·c²)` (weak-field).
- A `BE_XX_LENSING_LHS` AST encodes the deflection-angle integrand structurally; the v0.3.0/v0.3.5 dimensional-analysis machinery validates it.

**Numerical evaluator.** `evaluateBE_XX_LensingNumerical()`:
1. Set up the Schwarzschild metric at solar parameters (or chosen `M`, `b`).
2. Initial conditions for a null geodesic starting far away with impact parameter `b`, propagating inward, deflecting around the Sun, exiting on the other side.
3. RK4-integrate the null geodesic equation using the v0.4.0 `covariant-derivative`-driven geodesic integrator (8-dim state). Domain restricted to `r ≥ 3·r_Schwarz` and impact parameter `b ≫ r_Schwarz`.
4. Extract the asymptotic deflection angle from the trajectory.
5. Cross-check against `α = 4GM/(bc²)` to **±10⁻⁶ relative** (weak-field).

> **I6 reconciliation (2026-05-15):** the geodesic-integrated cross-validation lives at the end of `tests/bridges/gravitational-lensing.test.ts` ("BE-?? Gravitational Lensing — geodesic cross-validation"). v0.4.0 ships ±10⁻⁶ rather than the originally-promised ±10⁻⁹ — null short-arc RK4 with 100k steps reaches 1e-6 reliably; pushing to 1e-9 needs higher-order integrators (RK45 adaptive or symplectic) which are deferred to v0.5.0.

### BE-?? Mercury Perihelion Precession (secondary)

**Bridge identity.** Newtonian-Kepler-bound-orbit ↔ GR. Le Verrier (1859) measured the anomalous perihelion drift of Mercury — 43 arcsec/century in excess of Newtonian predictions — that Einstein (1915) explained as a GR effect: `Δφ_per_orbit = 6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²)`. For Mercury: `a ≈ 5.79·10¹⁰ m`, `e ≈ 0.206`, `M_sun = 1.989·10³⁰ kg` → `Δφ_per_orbit ≈ 5.04·10⁻⁷ rad` → ≈43 arcsec/century over ~415 Mercury years. Solar System's most famous early confirmation of GR.

**Symbolic encoding.** `src/bridges/equations/be-YY-perihelion-precession.ts`:
- Same Schwarzschild metric reuse.
- Encoded as the per-orbit secular change in the longitude-of-pericenter ω̇ of a bound timelike geodesic.
- Closed-form scalar: `Δφ_per_orbit = 6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²)`.

**Numerical evaluator.** `evaluateBE_YY_PerihelionNumerical()`:
1. Set up the Schwarzschild metric at Mercury-orbit-scale parameters.
2. Initial conditions for a bound timelike geodesic with given `a`, `e`, at perihelion.
3. RK4-integrate for one orbit (≥ 200k steps; multi-orbit accumulation deferred to v0.5.0). Domain restriction maintained throughout (`r ≫ r_Schwarz`).
4. Extract the perihelion-azimuth advance across the orbit.
5. Cross-check against `6πGM/(a(1−e²)c²)` to **±10⁻⁴ relative**.

> **I6 reconciliation (2026-05-15):** the geodesic-integrated cross-validation lives at the end of `tests/bridges/perihelion-precession.test.ts` ("BE-?? Mercury Perihelion — geodesic cross-validation"). v0.4.0 ships one-orbit ±10⁻⁴ rather than the originally-promised N-orbit ±10⁻⁹ — timelike multi-orbit RK4 accumulates error faster than null short-arc; pushing to 1e-9 needs higher-order integrators (symplectic Verlet/leapfrog) and adaptive step-size, deferred to v0.5.0.

### BE-37 covariant-eikonal preview (structural-equivalence test, tertiary)

A small structural test: `g^μν (∇_μ S)(∇_ν S) = 0` for the scalar `S_eikonal` from v0.3.5's BE-37. For scalar `of`, `∇_μ S = ∂_μ S` (Christoffel terms cancel). The numerical result must match v0.3.5's `evaluateBE37EikonalNumerical()` eikonal residual to **machine precision**. Confirms the Part-VIII §VIII.8 "only the operator changes" promise structurally.
