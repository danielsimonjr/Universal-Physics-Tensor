## What units already did, and what they refuse

Three nested lemmas are the Buckingham route at this pin. They are not kind `bridge`.

- be-15, [`Coarsening.length_monomial_at`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/Coarsening.lean), from [`Dimensional.monomial_form`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/Dimensional.lean). Form `L = C (Γ t)^{1/z}` under `[Γ] = L^z T⁻¹`. `z = 2` is not derived.
- be-17, [`EinsteinCartan.torsion_monomial`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/EinsteinCartan.lean). Form `T = C κ S`. `C = 1` is a hypothesis of the companion lemma, and [`coefficient_not_fixed`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/EinsteinCartan.lean) says a different factor is a different equation.
- be-33 is kind `bridge` by [`QuantumCritical.thermal_scaling`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/QuantumCritical.lean), which states `ξ(T) = ξ₀ (T/T₀)^{−1/z}` and is not Hertz–Millis. The nested [`scaling_shape`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/QuantumCritical.lean) and [`every_power_homogeneous`](https://github.com/danielsimonjr/PhysJS/blob/2e09357f9674bc60b60b378155a1623c27dc7b04/PhysJS/QuantumCritical.lean) only give `ξ = ξ₀ φ(T/T₀)` and the homogeneity of every real power. They do not choose the exponent. They are not a second reference.

No open row above becomes kind `bridge` by calling `monomial_form` again. Where the table says units give a monomial, the constant or the exponent stays a hypothesis, which is the same limitation those three lemmas already record.
